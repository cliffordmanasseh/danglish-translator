# Teacher quality audit, 2026-11-13

## What is being checked

The English side is generated by the local Qwen3.8 Flash teacher. Three
independent checks are used:

1. **Structural audit** — empty/copy/special tokens, truncation, length ratio,
   Danish residue, repeated 4-grams, missing numbers, glossary hits.
2. **Manual stratified audit** — read Danish and English side by side across
   decades and record meaning/omission/terminology errors.
3. **Round-trip audit** — translate the English back to Danish with an
   independent OPUS model and compare it to the source with chrF++.

The COMET-KIWI reference-free metric is gated on Hugging Face and was not
available, so the round-trip proxy is used instead. It catches omissions and
meaning drift, not every subtle theological error.

### Serious example (1782)

Source:

> Ingen af os holder Døden tilbage, gaaer Døden forbie, farer bort med Døden,
> saasom det lyster ham, og han selv har vildet anordne.

Teacher:

> None of us can hold back Death; when Death passes by, it sweeps away with it,
> just as it pleases him, and as he himself has willed to ordain.

The teacher lost the subject of “farer bort med Døden” (“we depart with
Death”). A better rendering: “None of us holds Death back; when Death passes
by, we depart with Death, as it pleases him, and as he himself has willed to
ordain.”

### Minor example (1944)

Source: “Aa, maatte han faa Lov til at drage det opad.”
Teacher: “Oh, may he be granted the grace to draw it upward.”

“faa Lov til” is “be allowed to”; “grace” is an added theological nuance.

## Interpretation

- **Modern sermons (2000s–2020s)** are strong enough to use at full scale. The
  structural filters and round-trip scores are clean, and the manual sample had
  no serious errors.
- **1940s sermons** are mostly good but use older orthography. Use them, but
  sample more before release; the round-trip score is low partly because the
  EN→DA model normalizes the old spelling.
- **1780s and earlier** have a higher semantic error rate. The teacher handles
  simple lines but loses complex archaic syntax. Either regenerate this subset
  with an archaic-Danish prompt, or keep it out of the main training mix.
- The structural audit is a tail-risk filter, not a quality guarantee. The
  manual sample is the real check; 15 pairs is too small to estimate a precise
  error rate.

## Planned actions

1. Rerun the structural audit at 5k and 20k generated rows.
2. Run the round-trip audit on a larger stratified sample and flag the bottom
   ~10% for manual review.
3. Decide on the historical subset after the larger sample:
   - keep the 1940s with a review pass,
   - regenerate the pre-1900 subset with a prompt that explicitly handles
     archaic Danish,
   - or exclude the pre-1900 subset from the student training mix.
4. Keep the 60-pair independent editor set as the unbiased **student**
   evaluation; teacher-referenced test scores remain a secondary signal.
